I'm a PhD student in Computer Science at [North Carolina State University](https://www.ncsu.edu/), where I work in the WISER Lab under the advisement of [Dr. Sarah Heckman](https://www.csc.ncsu.edu/people/sesmith5). My research interests are in **CS education** and **software engineering**.

Before NC State, I earned my B.S. in Computer Science (Software Engineering) from [CSU Monterey Bay](https://csumb.edu/), where I was deeply involved in teaching, mentoring, and undergraduate research on sustainable computing.

## What I Do

**Research.** I conduct CS education and software engineering research as a Graduate Research Assistant in the WISER Lab. My work explores how students learn and how we can build better tools, curricula, and pedagogies to support them. I also serve as a STEM Research Mentor at the Blackwell Research Institute, guiding students through scientific literature, research methods, and publication.

**Teach.** I'm the Lead Graduate TA for Software Engineering, leading lab instruction for 60+ students and periodically delivering full lectures. I also organize and lead GitHub portfolio workshops through NC State's ACM chapter and Computing Success Center. I hold both the NCSU Graduate Teaching Certificate and the Advanced Teaching Certificate.

**Build.** I volunteer as a mobile app developer with the NC State Helping Hands Project, building an iOS/Android application in React Native and TypeScript that connects to Arduino-powered prosthetic hands for children via Bluetooth Low Energy.
