I'm a PhD student in Computer Science at [North Carolina State University](https://www.ncsu.edu/), where I work in the [WISER Lab](https://www.csc.ncsu.edu/) under the advisement of [Dr. Sarah Heckman](https://www.csc.ncsu.edu/people/sesmith5). My research sits at the intersection of **CS education** and **software engineering** — I study how students learn to build software and how we can make that process more effective and equitable.

Before NC State, I earned my B.S. in Computer Science (Software Engineering) from [CSU Monterey Bay](https://csumb.edu/), where I was deeply involved in teaching, mentoring, and undergraduate research.

## What I Do

**Research.** As a Graduate Research Assistant in the WISER Lab, I study teaming, pedagogy, and tooling in CS education. My current work includes a systematic mapping study on teaming in CS education, under review at *ACM Transactions on Computing Education*. I've also mentored five high-school researchers through the Blackwell Research Institute, all of whom published in *The Blackwell Research Journal*.

**Teach.** I'm the Lead Graduate TA for CSC 326: Software Engineering at NC State, where I lead labs for 60+ students and deliver full lectures to 200+. I hold both the NC State Teaching Certificate and Advanced Teaching Certificate, and I organize GitHub portfolio workshops through NC State's ACM chapter and Computing Success Center.

**Build.** I contributed to the NC State Helping Hands Project as a mobile app developer, building an iOS/Android application in React Native and TypeScript with real-time Bluetooth Low Energy communication to Arduino-powered prosthetic hands for children.
