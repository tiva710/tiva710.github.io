I'm a PhD student in Computer Science at [North Carolina State University](https://www.ncsu.edu/), where I work in the WISER Lab under the advisement of Dr. Sarah Heckman. My research interests are in **CS education** and **software engineering**. 

Before NC State, I earned my BS in Computer Science (Software Engineering) from [CSU Monterey Bay](https://csumb.edu/), where I was deeply involved in teaching, mentoring, and undergraduate research on sustainable computing.

## What I Do

**Research.** I conduct CS education and software engineering research as a Graduate Research Assistant in the WISER Lab. My work explores how students learn and how we can build better tools, curricula, and pedagogies to support them.

**Teach.** I'm a Graduate TA for Software Engineering, leading lab instruction for 60+ students. I also organize and lead GitHub portfolio workshops through NC State's Computing Success Center and ACM chapter. I hold an NCSU Graduate Teaching Certificate, with the Advanced certificate in progress.

**Mentor.** I serve as a mentor through NC State's Women in Computer Science program and as a STEM tutor and research mentor at the Blackwell Research Institute.
